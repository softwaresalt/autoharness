## Why the mutating run was not invoked

The duration estimate comes from backlogit's own per-artifact event log
(`.backlogit/logs/*.jsonl`) for the two most recent cascades on this
workspace:

| Phase | 201-S (12 artifacts) | 202-S (10 artifacts) |

The model is about 133 s fixed plus about 35 s per artifact. It predicts 553 s
for 201-S (actual 581 s) and 483 s for 202-S (actual ~486 s). The per-archive
cost tracks a full index rebuild: `backlogit sync` measured 25.45 s on this
workspace just before the run. For 198-S (23 manifest items plus the shipment
record, so 24 artifacts), the prediction is 133 + 24 × 35 ≈ 973 s. With the
+5 % error seen on 201-S, it is about 1020 s. Both exceed the 900 s
`MAX_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` cap. The command's default `--timeout` is 120 s, which
every recent cascade on this workspace would also have exceeded.

On a timeout, `run_bounded` kills the process group
(`src/autoharness/shipment_close/runner.py`). At about 900 s the engine would
be roughly 20 archives in, so the backlog would be torn, the command would exit
6, and re-running, committing, or substituting `SAFE_CLOSE` would be forbidden
until an operator reviews it. Stopping before invocation leaves the backlog
untouched and the `pre_close` record valid.
