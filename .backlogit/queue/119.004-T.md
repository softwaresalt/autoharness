H7 ANTI-DRIFT (explicit, test-enforced): the ONLY permitted subscribers in this feature are the journal writer and the console renderer. No background thread performs verification, summarization, or compaction - this event bus is a HOOK SURFACE that a future candidate (c) Verification and Compaction layer could consume, but candidate (c) is NOT implemented here and Engram gains NO authority. A test asserts supervise/ contains no bind/listen call and imports nothing from gradio, fastapi, flask, uvicorn, aiohttp, or a devtunnel client. Local in-process fan-out only - no sockets, no IPC, no network.
