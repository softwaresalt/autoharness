Determine REQUIRED FINDING F7 (question Q7): what TOOL AUTHORITY the .mcp.json registration entry for an autoharness server grants. Three parts, and answering fewer does not answer it: (1) the LITERAL per-tool allowlist value, written exactly as it would be committed; (2) the PROJECTION RULE deriving that allowlist from the operation registry, stated as a rule rather than a one-time list, so a newly registered operation becomes or fails to become callable by rule; (3) the OBSERVED default for a registered-but-not-allowlisted operation, which must be NOT CALLABLE (fail closed) - and if the prototype shows any other default, that is itself the finding and is recorded as one. WILDCARD AUTHORITY IS REJECTED. "tools": ["*"] may be recommended only if this task records all three of: a specific capability no enumerated allowlist can express; the exact bound replacing enumeration, as a checkable predicate; and why that bound is not weaker than enumeration, in falsifiable terms. Absent all three, the recommendation is an enumerated allowlist and the wildcard is recorded as REJECTED WITH ITS REASON. A wildcard that is merely convenient, merely the prototype default, or merely consistent with the existing backlogit entry is rejected on each ground separately.
