Wire the historical-record classification into the STARTUP RECOVERY SCAN CONTRACT in the agent templates and their installed mirrors.

The scan enumerates records and classifies each one through the single predicate with origin="historical". The contract text must encode BOTH branches explicitly: status: resolved with no usable hint is reported as CHECKPOINT_LEGACY_HINTLESS_RESOLVED and EXCLUDED from the candidate set; status: active with a missing, empty or whitespace-only hint yields the BLOCKING CHECKPOINT_ACTIVE_HINTLESS and FAILS CLOSED to operator handoff. The scan reports the legacy set by filename and count so the exemption is visible rather than silent.

NO RECORD IS LEFT UNCLASSIFIED. The contract states that the classification is total and deterministic over the corpus, so a reader cannot infer a silent-pass path for a record that matches neither branch cleanly.

The templates reference the predicate; they DO NOT RE-STATE THE RULE INLINE. A per-agent restatement produces two drifting definitions of the same rule, which is the failure mode the single-predicate design exists to prevent.

Blocked by 172.001-T: the scan cannot reference a classification the policy has not defined. Blocks 172.008-T. Scope: templates/agents/ plus the installed .github/agents/ mirrors; all copies change and revert together.
