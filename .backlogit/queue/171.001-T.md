Define the durable plan identity schema that makes "the single governing plan" a machine-decidable property. Adds to the plan frontmatter contract: plan_id (stable across renames and revisions), revision (monotonic integer), status (active | superseded), supersedes (plan_id + revision of the predecessor, or null), and superseded_by. THE SINGLE-ACTIVE CONSTRAINT KEYS ON plan_id, NOT ON FILE PATH: at most one document may carry status: active for a given plan_id, so renaming or copying the file cannot manufacture a second governing plan. Emits PLAN_IDENTITY_MISSING when required identity fields are absent and PLAN_MULTIPLE_ACTIVE when two or more active documents share a plan_id. Scope: schemas/ and the plan frontmatter definition only; no verifier code (that is 171.006-T) and no tests (those are 171.015-T). Entry point of 179-S: no predecessors. Blocks 171.004-T, 171.005-T and 171.015-T. IN-SCOPE BOUNDARY: this task defines identity for plans authored from here forward. It does NOT migrate existing append-only plans -- that surface is deferred to 175.003-T and nothing in 179-S depends on it. Stash C9CD24F3.
