DOCUMENT THE OPERATOR-ONLY, APPROVAL-GATED ADMINISTRATIVE-CLOSE PROCEDURE IN FULL - the four binding constraints PLUS the seven-step procedure. This is documentation of an operator procedure; no agent performs it autonomously.

THE FOUR BINDING CONSTRAINTS: the procedure is OPERATOR-ONLY and never agent-initiated; it is APPROVAL-GATED per invocation; it NEVER forges an upstream-supported transition (it records an administrative disposition, it does not pretend the refused transition succeeded); and it is AUDITED, leaving evidence sufficient for a later reader to reconstruct exactly what changed and why.

THE SEVEN-STEP PROCEDURE:
1. FRESH IN-SESSION APPROVAL naming the EXACT SHIPMENT ID, its CURRENT STATE, the EXACT FIELDS to be written, and the EXACT EFFECTS of writing them. A standing or prior-session approval does not satisfy this; an approval that does not name the current state cannot be given against the state that actually exists.
2. BYTE SNAPSHOT of every record to be modified, plus MANIFEST AND RELATIONSHIP HASHES, taken before any mutation. The hashes are what make a later rollback verifiable rather than hopeful.
3. MANIFEST-SCOPE VERIFICATION: confirm the shipment's manifest is exactly its covering feature's descendant set, so the close cannot silently act on membership that drifted.
4. THE EXACT MECHANISM: a FIXED ARGUMENT VECTOR with shell=False through the official backlogit operation. DIRECT FILE EDITING IS PROHIBITED - hand-editing a record bypasses the tool's own invariants and produces a state no reader can trust.
5. FIVE ASSERTED POSTCONDITIONS verified after the mutation, covering the written fields, the shipment's resulting state, manifest membership unchanged, relationship hashes accounted for, and no collateral record modified.
6. AUTOMATIC ROLLBACK ON ANY FAILED POSTCONDITION, restoring from the byte snapshot and re-verifying the hashes. Rollback is automatic and total, not an operator judgement call after the fact.
7. AN AUDIT / P-005 EVIDENCE RECORD capturing the approval text, the coordinates acted on, the mechanism invoked, the postcondition results, and the rollback outcome if any.
