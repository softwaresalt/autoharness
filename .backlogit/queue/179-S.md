Establishes the minimum single-governing-plan contract: durable plan identity with a per-plan_id single-active constraint, one immutable artifact per review attempt, explicit latest-attempt/verdict selection via a structured manifest, regenerate-not-patch remediation with acyclic path-valued supersession, and a pre-dispatch verifier over six blocking tokens plus one reported signal.

ONE NORMATIVE WIRE FORMAT, NO SECOND VOCABULARY. Plan identity is exactly seven fields: plan_id, plan_role (active | superseded | history, never status), revision, supersedes and superseded_by as repo-relative paths or null, source_history as an ordered path list, review_manifest as a path. The verdict manifest is exactly eight keys: plan_id, plan_path, plan_revision, latest_attempt, verdict, latest_artifact, attempts[], carried_forward_context[] (always present, possibly empty). Roster entries are {attempt, artifact, reviewed_revision, verdict, remediation_revision, disposition}, keeping reviewer judgement and Stage remediation separate; REMEDIATED-PENDING-REVIEW is a disposition and never a verdict. plan_revision_reviewed is not a field name in this contract.

LEGACY BOUNDARY IS MECHANICAL: the PLAN_LEGACY_UNIDENTIFIED exemption applies only to plans on the immutable committed allowlist schemas/plan-legacy-allowlist.json, generated once at contract introduction. Off-allowlist omission is blocking PLAN_IDENTITY_MISSING. A date cutoff was rejected as author-controlled.

SCOPE REDUCED: plan budgets, compact-context auto-trigger, harvest rewiring and repository-wide migration are DEFERRED to feature 175-F, not dropped. Every descoped child was re-homed out of 171-F into 175-F via backlogit adopt, so the 179-S manifest and the live descendant set of 171-F are IDENTICAL -- eleven tasks plus the covering feature, with no live blocked child left inside the covering feature. 179-S can therefore close normally without implementing deferred scope and without a SAFE_CLOSE disposition. NO IN-MANIFEST TASK DEPENDS ON DESCOPED WORK, and PLAN_BUDGET_BREACH appears in no shipped artifact.
