Establishes the single-governing-plan contract: durable plan identity with a per-plan_id single-active constraint, one immutable artifact per numeric review attempt, deterministic latest-attempt/verdict selection through a structured manifest, regenerate-not-patch remediation with acyclic path-valued supersession, and a pre-dispatch verifier over EIGHT blocking tokens plus one reported signal.

EXPLICIT PYTHON MODULE OWNERSHIP. Six modules under src/autoharness/planreview/ each own one concern: paths.py (canonical workspace-contained resolution; REVIEW_PATH_ESCAPE), manifest.py (eight-key wire format, integer attempts, legacy normalization), inputset.py + assembler.py (assembly only, emits no token), history.py (the sole naming-independent historical-document predicate; REVIEW_INPUT_HISTORY_LEAK), remediation.py (write_next_revision, link_supersession), verifier.py (six tokens implemented, two delegated). Markdown plan-review and impl-plan surfaces CONSUME those modules through the autoharness plan-review CLI and restate no rule; no task scope broadens into a module it does not own.

EIGHT BLOCKING TOKENS reach a consumer: PLAN_MULTIPLE_ACTIVE, PLAN_IDENTITY_MISSING, PLAN_SUPERSEDES_CYCLE, REVIEW_VERDICT_AMBIGUOUS, REVIEW_VERDICT_MISSING, REVIEW_ATTEMPT_DUPLICATE from verifier.py, plus delegated REVIEW_INPUT_HISTORY_LEAK and REVIEW_PATH_ESCAPE. PLAN_LEGACY_UNIDENTIFIED is reported and non-blocking, only for plans on the immutable schemas/plan-legacy-allowlist.json generated once at contract introduction; off-allowlist omission blocks with PLAN_IDENTITY_MISSING. A date cutoff was rejected as author-controlled.

ONE NORMATIVE WIRE FORMAT. Plan identity is exactly seven fields: plan_id, plan_role (active | superseded), revision, supersedes and superseded_by as repo-relative paths or null, source_history as an ordered path list, review_manifest as a path. The verdict manifest is exactly eight keys: plan_id, plan_path, plan_revision, latest_attempt, verdict, latest_artifact, attempts[], carried_forward_context[]. Roster entries are {attempt, artifact, reviewed_revision, verdict, remediation_revision, disposition}, keeping reviewer judgement separate from Stage remediation; REMEDIATED-PENDING-REVIEW is a disposition, never a verdict.

LEGACY RECONCILIATION WITHOUT REWRITING IMMUTABLE HISTORY. Attempts are INTEGERS. The live combined 01-02 artifact is represented as numeric attempt 2 carrying legacy_coverage {covers_attempts, original_attempt_label, note} and form combined-document. The live multipart SAFE_CLOSE attempt 6 is represented by ONE immutable index artifact with form multipart-index and a parts[] list naming the originals, one authoritative_for_attempt. Originals are byte-untouched. Exactly one authoritative artifact per numeric attempt yields deterministic latest selection, and ALL SIX live manifests are fixtures.

TEST-FIRST DAG. Three RED entry points precede every implementation surface and two GREEN observation tasks follow. RED tests are IMPORT-SAFE: not-yet-existing boundaries are loaded inside the test body, so each test individually executes and fails on its assertion rather than collapsing at collection into a missing observation.

THE PRODUCER CLOSES THE LOOP: impl-plan emits the seven-field revision-1 identity and initializes the verdict manifest at authoring time, so a freshly authored plan passes the verifier on its FIRST review with no manual metadata step.

DEFERRED to feature 175-F and NOT dropped: plan budgets, compact-context auto-trigger, harvest rewiring and the repository-wide migration of pre-existing plans. Every descoped child was re-homed out of 171-F into 175-F, so this manifest is IDENTICAL to the live descendant set of 171-F and 179-S closes normally without a SAFE_CLOSE disposition. NO IN-MANIFEST TASK DEPENDS ON DEFERRED SCOPE, and PLAN_BUDGET_BREACH appears in no shipped artifact.
