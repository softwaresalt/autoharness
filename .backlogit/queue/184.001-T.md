SCOPE (test-only, single file): in tests/test_capability_pack_enforcement_verifier.py, replace the bare `with tempfile.TemporaryDirectory() as temp_dir:` teardown in the module-level _run() helper (line ~114) with a bounded, re-raising retry around the temp-workspace removal. Catch PermissionError whose `winerror` is 32 (sharing violation - file in use by another process) or 5 (access denied); retry the removal a SMALL FIXED number of times with a short bounded backoff; and RE-RAISE the final exception once the bound is exhausted. Any PermissionError with a different winerror, and any other OSError, MUST propagate immediately without retry. Every existing test in the module, and every assertion, stays byte-for-byte unchanged in intent and count.

WHY (evidence): after 622a41a1 the canonical suite failed TWICE at CapabilityPackEnforcementVerifierTests::test_orphaned_manifest_entry_without_pack_fails inside tempfile.TemporaryDirectory.__exit__ -> shutil.rmtree, not in an assertion. A read-only concurrency review of src/autoharness/verify_workspace.py (capability_pack_enforcement targeted check) found NO production file-handle leak, which is what makes this repair test-only.

PROHIBITED (each of these fails the task):

A bounded retry that still re-raises preserves the signal; a swallowing cleanup makes a persistent handle leak indistinguishable from a healthy run and permanently hides the WinError 5 access-denied case.
- An unbounded retry, an unbounded/growing backoff, or a retry that ends in `pass`/`return` instead of a raise.
- Any change under src/ - notably src/autoharness/verify_workspace.py. Production code is out of scope by evidence.
- Any change to another test module, including tests/test_graphtor_mcp_shim.py. The Graphtor tearDown child-process/pipe-handle leak is captured SEPARATELY as stash 8CB606F8 (P2 follow-up) and MUST NOT be fixed here, even if step (2) or (3) below implicates it.

- Adding a `dir=Path.cwd()` anchor to TemporaryDirectory. tests/test_test_suite_isolation_contract.py guards against reintroducing that pattern anywhere under tests/ (docs/closure/149-S-141-F-post-merge-closure.md); doing so would regress that guard immediately.
