backlogit v1.11.0 (commit 5a4b70dd, 174.058-T) made `shipment ship` flat: collectArchiveCandidateIDs archives only the shipment, terminal explicit members, and explicit feature members; linked deliberations and unlisted descendants remain independent. autoharness still encodes the 1.10.x behavior (linked deliberations in allowed_ids/required_ids), so every CASCADE close with a live linked deliberation halts post-mutation (190-S / 034-DL) and SAFE_CLOSE silently strands the same deliberation.

Chosen remedy (deliberation 038-DL, Option B): (1) realign P-015 D1a/INV-1/INV-6, the shipment-reconcile Cascade Close sets, the Ship agent CASCADE bullet and the shipment_closure.py docstring to flat semantics — allowed_ids(S)=closure_scope(S); a linked deliberation in archived_ids is an engine-drift HALT and must be byte-identical after the cascade; (2) new path-independent Linked-Deliberation Disposition step (INV-12) after either close path: shared-reference guard, then individual non-cascade archive with hash re-check and verify-after-each; (3) CASCADE additionally requires a fresh backlogit version probe inside the verified engine-semantics line (1.11, single-source constant VERIFIED_CASCADE_ENGINE_MINOR_LINES), otherwise SAFE_CLOSE with ENGINE_SEMANTICS_UNVERIFIED.
