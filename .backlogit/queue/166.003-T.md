CHANGES
1. REWRITE the P-015 Statement in flat-manifest terms: a shipment manifest is a FLAT MANIFEST of exactly what is delivered. Closure scope is the manifest's explicit item IDs PLUS the shipment record. A manifest is NOT a container and carries NO implicit hierarchical closure semantics. ANCESTRY NEVER EXPANDS CLOSURE SCOPE.
2. REPLACE the "VERIFIED FULLY-COVERED-ROOT EXCEPTION" heading and its preconditions 1-5 with INV-1..INV-10, transcribed verbatim from docs/decisions/2026-09-15-flat-manifest-shipment-closure-deliberation.md section D1. Retain precondition 7's two-set allowed_ids/required_ids gate and its 155-S supersession note unchanged — that gate is ALREADY flat-manifest-shaped and remains the post-mutation guard (INV-10).
3. REWRITE the Precondition and Required Check paragraphs from baseline-PRESENCE to baseline-INVARIANCE (INV-7): record each out-of-manifest artifact's baseline location and content hash; require that NOTHING outside closure scope CHANGES during the run. An artifact already archived, descoped, or missing AT BASELINE is recorded as baseline state and is explicitly NOT a halt — it carries no signal about THIS run.
4. STATE INV-8 EXPLICITLY (plan-review P1-1): manifest ORDERING (feature last in the final shipment) is a STAGE ASSEMBLY convention. Ship MUST NOT evaluate manifest ordering as a closure precondition. Without this, every historical manifest — all of which list the covering feature FIRST, including 173-S — would be invalidated.
5. STATE INV-4/INV-5: a feature MAY be decomposed across an ordered sequence of shipments; intermediate shipments carry NO feature member; exactly one shipment (the final) carries the feature item, placed last. Only tasks ACTUALLY DELIVERED by the sequence are subject to the terminality precondition.
6. STATE INV-3 in normative prose: items excluded, removed, descoped, rejected, merged into other work, or otherwise absent from a manifest MUST NEVER block the feature record or the shipment record from reaching a terminal state ON THE GROUND OF ANCESTRY. No disposition note, no archived_status value, and no provenance may be demanded of such an artifact.

ADD a SUPERSESSION NOTE (2026-09-15) recording: the operator architectural correction; the withdrawal of the hierarchical-closure preconditions; the withdrawal of TERMINAL_CLOSE and the terminal-descope exemption; and the superseded decision path.
