CROSS-SURFACE LINKAGE HALF ONLY. Implement supersession bookkeeping across the three surfaces once 171.005-T has produced a revision N+1 document.

(1) Set the predecessor revision's plan_role to superseded and populate its superseded_by with the REPO-RELATIVE PATH of the new revision. (2) Populate the new revision's supersedes with the REPO-RELATIVE PATH of the predecessor, and append the consumed immutable review artifact to source_history. supersedes and superseded_by are PATHS OR null, never (plan_id, revision) pairs, because PLAN_SUPERSEDES_CYCLE fires on "cyclic OR UNRESOLVABLE" and unresolvability presupposes a resolvable reference. (3) Roll the verdict manifest's eight keys - plan_id, plan_path, plan_revision, latest_attempt, verdict, latest_artifact, attempts[], carried_forward_context[] - so the selection surface points at the correct current revision.

ROSTER WRITES KEEP REVIEW AND REMEDIATION SEPARATE. The new attempts[] entry records reviewed_revision and verdict for what the INDEPENDENT REVIEWER judged, and remediation_revision and disposition for what STAGE produced in response. REMEDIATED-PENDING-REVIEW is written as a disposition and is NEVER written as a verdict. The top-level verdict is then RECOMPUTED from the roster rather than authored: if the highest-numbered entry's remediation_revision equals plan_revision, it is that entry's disposition, otherwise that entry's verdict.

MUST BE ACYCLIC: emit PLAN_SUPERSEDES_CYCLE if the resulting supersedes chain is not a strictly decreasing path terminating at a revision whose supersedes is null, or if any edge fails to resolve to a document.
