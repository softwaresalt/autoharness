PRODUCER 1 OF 2 - the Stage checkpoint author guarantee.

Every Stage-authored checkpoint emits a top-level resume_hint specific enough to support a later recovery decision. A hint must name the next actionable step or state explicitly that no re-entry is required; a generic or empty string does not satisfy the guarantee. THIS EXPLICITLY INCLUDES THE MINIMAL END-OF-SESSION COMPLETION CHECKPOINT, which is the shape the historical gap was first observed in - a completion checkpoint is exactly the case an author is most likely to treat as not needing a hint.
