Define the structured latest-verdict manifest record: the single mutable selection surface at docs/reviews/{plan-slug}-plan-review.md that names, in machine-readable frontmatter, which immutable attempt artifact is authoritative.

THE MANIFEST CARRIES EXACTLY EIGHT KEYS and no others: plan_id; plan_path (repo-relative path to the plan_role: active document); plan_revision (the revision currently governing); latest_attempt; verdict; latest_artifact (repo-relative path to the immutable artifact for latest_attempt); attempts[]; carried_forward_context[].

ROSTER ENTRY SHAPE - REVIEW AND REMEDIATION ARE SEPARATE FACTS. Each attempts[] entry is {attempt, artifact, reviewed_revision, verdict, remediation_revision, disposition}. reviewed_revision plus verdict describe what an INDEPENDENT REVIEWER judged; remediation_revision plus disposition describe what STAGE subsequently produced. Recording a Stage-produced revision under verdict would assert a review that never happened, and a roster carrying only one revision number cannot distinguish "the reviewer passed revision 6" from "the reviewer blocked revision 5 and Stage emitted revision 6". verdict is PASS | BLOCKED only. REMEDIATED-PENDING-REVIEW IS NEVER A ROSTER verdict VALUE - it is a disposition, because Stage does not review its own remediation. remediation_revision and disposition are both null when no remediation followed.

TOP-LEVEL verdict IS DERIVED, NOT AUTHORED: take the highest-numbered roster entry; if its remediation_revision equals plan_revision, top-level verdict is that entry's disposition, otherwise it is that entry's verdict. Its enum is PASS | BLOCKED | REMEDIATED-PENDING-REVIEW. This makes a fabricated headline a detectable inconsistency rather than a matter of narrative.

carried_forward_context[] is a list of {artifact, reason} entries and is ALWAYS PRESENT, possibly empty but never absent; an undefined input is either silently empty, making any requirement stated over it vacuous, or improvised per caller.

EXPLICIT SELECTION, NEVER INFERENCE: no consumer may derive "the latest attempt" by sorting filenames, reading the longest file, or taking the last heading in an appended document.

Defines the two ambiguity tokens: REVIEW_VERDICT_AMBIGUOUS (two records claim latest, or the authored top-level verdict disagrees with the roster-derived one) and REVIEW_VERDICT_MISSING (the manifest names an attempt with no record, or carries no verdict field).
