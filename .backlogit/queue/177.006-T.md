COVERAGE AND COMPOSED-STATE GATE (decision D6). Attempt 01 found that the plan's declared pass state was unreachable because a declared property had no determining task, and that the previous hardening pass never asked whether one existed. This task makes the coverage check STRUCTURAL rather than incidental, and executes the state transition mechanically so the gate is a check rather than a claim. TWO CHECKS, IN ORDER. (1) COVERAGE: every property I1-I7 has an assigned DETERMINING task in the plan's Property coverage table, and that task is a determining task - 177.004-T, 177.005-T, 177.001-T or 177.002-T - NEVER the authoring task 177.003-T alone. A property whose only assignment is the authoring task FAILS THIS CHECK OUTRIGHT, because an authoring task cannot produce an observation and an authoring-derived verdict rests on assertion, which R3 and H2 have already ruled insufficient. (2) EVIDENCE: resolve every property's row in the coverage ledger of docs/spikes/2026-09-18-conformance-isolation-findings.md to exactly one of three values. DETERMINED - a verdict is present AND the evidence shape named in the Property coverage table is present in the artifact. NOT DETERMINED - FLOOR INVOKED - the determining task reached its elapsed bound or was blocked AND the artifact records ALL THREE of what was attempted, what blocked it, and an explicit written statement that the property is UNPROVEN and that 181-S is held at the evidence-and-documentation floor for that property's scope. ABSENT - anything else, including a verdict supported only by a documentation citation and including a bare "not determined" carrying fewer than all three parts. THEN EMIT EXACTLY ONE OF FOUR COMPOSED STATES, and append a single verdict line to the findings artifact. ISOLATION_CHARACTERIZED, when every property is DETERMINED - "COMPOSED_STATE: ISOLATION_CHARACTERIZED | determined=7 | floor_invoked=0 | absent=0 | checked=<date>". THIS IS THE ONLY PASS STATE. ISOLATION_FLOOR_ONLY, when no property is ABSENT and at least one is NOT DETERMINED - FLOOR INVOKED - "COMPOSED_STATE: ISOLATION_FLOOR_ONLY | determined=<n> | floor_invoked=<m> | absent=0 | floor_properties=I<x>,I<y> | checked=<date>". THIS IS NOT A PASS: it records that the named properties are UNPROVEN, and it asserts NOTHING about the isolation. ISOLATION_UNDETERMINED, when one or more properties is ABSENT - "COMPOSED_STATE: ISOLATION_UNDETERMINED | absent=<n> | properties=I<x>,I<y>". ISOLATION_NOT_OBSERVED - the findings artifact does not exist, or exists with no completed coverage ledger or no verdict line; distinct from ISOLATION_UNDETERMINED and NEVER a pass. SUCCESSOR ELIGIBILITY IS STATED, NOT INFERRED: under ISOLATION_CHARACTERIZED, 181-S may harvest every task the findings support; under ISOLATION_FLOOR_ONLY, 181-S may harvest ONLY the evidence-and-documentation floor tasks and NO task that assumes, relies on or asserts an unproven property; under ISOLATION_UNDETERMINED and ISOLATION_NOT_OBSERVED, 181-S may harvest NOTHING. Attempt 02 finding F2 (P2) found the previous revision asserting that ISOLATION_UNDETERMINED both blocked 181-S harvest and left the degradation floor standing, which cannot both be operative; the four-state table above replaces that pair with a per-state eligibility rule. FAIL CLOSED: any row not affirmatively DETERMINED or NOT DETERMINED - FLOOR INVOKED is ABSENT, and any missing, unparseable or verdict-less artifact is ISOLATION_NOT_OBSERVED. Silence never produces eligibility, and NO state presents the isolation floor as PROVEN - 002-C stays blocked under all four.
