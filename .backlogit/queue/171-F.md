Establish a SINGLE GOVERNING PLAN CONTRACT so that, for any plan_id, exactly one plan document is active, its review history is immutable and deterministically indexed, and remediation regenerates the plan at revision + 1 instead of appending a correction log.

EXPLICIT PYTHON MODULE OWNERSHIP is the organizing constraint. Six modules under src/autoharness/planreview/ each own exactly one concern and no other surface restates its rule: paths.py (canonical workspace-contained resolution, REVIEW_PATH_ESCAPE), manifest.py (the eight-key wire format, integer attempts, legacy normalization), inputset.py + assembler.py (assembly only, emits no token), history.py (the sole naming-independent historical-document predicate and REVIEW_INPUT_HISTORY_LEAK), remediation.py (write_next_revision and link_supersession), and verifier.py (six blocking tokens implemented, two delegated). Markdown plan-review and impl-plan surfaces CONSUME those modules through the autoharness plan-review CLI; they restate no rule and a task scope never silently broadens to a module it does not own.

EIGHT BLOCKING TOKENS reach a consumer: PLAN_MULTIPLE_ACTIVE, PLAN_IDENTITY_MISSING, PLAN_SUPERSEDES_CYCLE, REVIEW_VERDICT_AMBIGUOUS, REVIEW_VERDICT_MISSING, REVIEW_ATTEMPT_DUPLICATE (from verifier.py) plus delegated REVIEW_INPUT_HISTORY_LEAK and REVIEW_PATH_ESCAPE. PLAN_LEGACY_UNIDENTIFIED is reported, non-blocking, and only for a plan on the immutable schemas/plan-legacy-allowlist.json.

LEGACY RECONCILIATION WITHOUT REWRITING HISTORY: the six live manifests already carry a combined 01-02 artifact and a multipart SAFE_CLOSE attempt 6. Both are normalized to INTEGER attempts - the combined document becomes numeric attempt 2 with explicit legacy_coverage metadata, and the multipart attempt becomes ONE immutable index artifact referencing the originals as parts. Originals are never edited. Exactly one authoritative artifact per numeric attempt yields deterministic latest selection, and all six live manifests are fixtures.

TEST-FIRST. Three RED entry points (171.016-T schema/wire-format/six-manifest normalization, 171.017-T module-boundary and path resolver, 171.015-T producer and eight-token verifier contract) precede every implementation surface, and three GREEN tasks (171.019-T, 171.011-T and the module-boundary regressions inside it) follow. RED tests are IMPORT-SAFE: not-yet-existing boundaries are imported inside the test body, never at module scope, so each test individually executes and fails on its assertion rather than collapsing at collection into a missing observation.

THE PRODUCER CLOSES THE LOOP: impl-plan emits the seven-field revision-1 identity and initializes the verdict manifest at authoring time (171.021-T), so a freshly authored plan passes the verifier on its FIRST review with no manual metadata step.

DEFERRED AND OUT OF SCOPE: the repository-wide migration of pre-existing plans and its regression suite are not in this feature and NO in-manifest task depends on them.
