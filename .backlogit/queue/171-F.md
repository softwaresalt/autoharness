title: Single-governing-plan contract with immutable review history
updated_at: 2026-09-18T20:19:58.7005094Z

Make "the single governing plan" a machine-decidable property, and make review history immutable and explicitly selectable, so that a plan-review loop can converge instead of re-reading its own past.

REDUCED SCOPE: this feature delivers ONLY the minimum contract -- durable plan identity with a per-plan_id single-active constraint bounded by an immutable legacy allowlist, one immutable artifact per review attempt, a structured latest-verdict manifest selected explicitly rather than inferred, a regenerate-not-patch remediation path with acyclic path-valued supersession bookkeeping, and a pre-dispatch verifier over six blocking tokens plus one non-blocking classification signal.

ONE NORMATIVE WIRE FORMAT GOVERNS EVERY SURFACE. Plan identity is exactly seven fields: plan_id; plan_role (active | superseded | history, and NEVER status, which plan frontmatter already uses for draft | reviewed); revision; supersedes and superseded_by as REPO-RELATIVE PATHS or null; source_history as an ordered list of paths; review_manifest as a path. The verdict manifest is exactly eight keys: plan_id, plan_path, plan_revision, latest_attempt, verdict, latest_artifact, attempts[], carried_forward_context[]. plan_revision_reviewed is not a field name in this contract.

EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE AND DEFERRED TO 175-F: repository-wide plan migration (175.003-T) and its regression suite (175.004-T), the plan budget contract and PLAN_BUDGET_BREACH token (stash 95575B96), compact-context auto-consolidation (175.001-T), and harvest rewiring for atomic plan references (175.002-T). No task in this feature may depend on, reference as a gate, or emit a token belonging to any of those surfaces.

LEGACY DOCUMENTS ARE CLASSIFIED, NOT CONVERTED, AND THE EXEMPTION IS BOUNDED: the non-blocking PLAN_LEGACY_UNIDENTIFIED signal applies only to plans on the immutable committed allowlist schemas/plan-legacy-allowlist.json. A newly authored plan that omits identity is off-allowlist and BLOCKS with PLAN_IDENTITY_MISSING, so the exemption cannot become a permanent bypass. This is why the verifier can enforce at blocking severity without waiting on deferred migration work.
