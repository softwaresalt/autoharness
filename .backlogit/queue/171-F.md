Make "the single governing plan" a machine-decidable property, and make review history immutable and explicitly selectable, so that a plan-review loop can converge instead of re-reading its own past. REDUCED SCOPE (plan revision 3 finding 5, carried into revision 4): this feature delivers ONLY the minimum contract -- durable plan identity with a per-plan_id single-active constraint, one immutable artifact per review attempt, a structured latest-verdict manifest selected explicitly rather than inferred, a regenerate-not-patch remediation path with acyclic supersession bookkeeping, and a pre-dispatch verifier over six blocking tokens plus one non-blocking classification signal.
