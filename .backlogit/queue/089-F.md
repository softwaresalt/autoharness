Follow-up hardening for two Copilot review findings against PR #229's throwaway,
disabled-by-default 088-F compression experiment (`experiments/088-compression-experiment/`).
Both findings are isolated to that experiment module; nothing in `src/autoharness`
depends on it (see `docs/compound/093-S-review-loop-convergence.md`).

1. `brainspace/workspace.py::resolve_workspace_root` — a truthy non-string
   `payload["cwd"]` (e.g. `{"cwd": [...]}`) passes the `isinstance(dict)` and
   truthiness guards then reaches `os.path.realpath()`, raising an uncaught
   `TypeError` that crashes the hook instead of the required fail-safe `{}`
   passthrough (`hook_cli.py` only catches `WorkspaceContainmentError`).
2. `brainspace/benchmark.py::_run_compression_case` — the early-decline
   return path omits `capture_failed`/`provenance` from its `CaseResult`,
   unlike the full (non-declined) path, creating an evidence-report honesty
   gap for the benchmark's declared invariant.

Plan: `docs/plans/2026-07-26-088-f-review-followup-hardening-plan.md` (plan-review
gate: PASS).

<!-- BEGIN:dod -->
- `resolve_workspace_root` raises `WorkspaceContainmentError` (not an uncaught
  `TypeError`) for a dict payload whose `cwd` is a truthy non-string value,
  proven by a red-then-green test, with a negative control proving a valid
  string `cwd` still resolves.
- `_run_compression_case`'s early-decline `CaseResult` carries `capture_failed`
  and `provenance` evidence through to `criteria`/`notes`, matching the full
  path's reporting shape, proven by red-then-green tests for both
  `capture_failed=True` and non-`"live"` provenance.
- Neither fix weakens decide-then-stash, byte-equivalent retrieval, or
  secret-screen-before-store invariants.
- `pytest` green for `test_workspace_resolution.py` and
  `test_benchmark_runner.py`.
