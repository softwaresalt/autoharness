SIZE: M. COMPLEXITY: medium. (Two independent axes; also emitted as structured backlog fields.) Shipment 3 (P1). Create src/autoharness/supervise/sidecar.py owning sidecar lifecycle/preflight currently duplicated in start.ps1 and start.sh: `backlogit sync` when backlogit is present, and Engram pre-warm as DIRECT sync first with a DAEMON `bind` + daemon-sync FALLBACK on failure. Returns a typed SidecarReport with a per-sidecar outcome of ok | degraded | unavailable, plus warnings. FAILURE IS NON-FATAL, matching today's behavior, but is NEVER silently swallowed - every degradation produces a warning and a SidecarProbed event. All invocations use argv arrays with resolved absolute executable paths; no shell (H4). CRITICAL (compound learning 2026-08-08-state-vs-call-outcome-conflation-in-telemetry-mapping): a sidecar's reported STATE is NOT the supervisor call's own outcome - a degraded Engram must remain a per-sidecar outcome and must never be mapped onto the supervisor's status. AUTHORITY (H6): Engram stays READ-ONLY with no execution or mutation authority and no supervisor decision may depend on it; backlogit and graphtor are not mutated by this service.
