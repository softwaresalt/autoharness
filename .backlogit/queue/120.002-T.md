SIZE: M. COMPLEXITY: medium. (Two independent axes; also emitted as structured backlog fields.) Shipment 3 (P1). Create src/autoharness/supervise/sidecar.py owning sidecar lifecycle/preflight currently duplicated in start.ps1 and start.sh: `backlogit sync` when backlogit is present, and Engram pre-warm as DIRECT sync first with a DAEMON `bind` + daemon-sync FALLBACK on failure. Returns a typed SidecarReport with a per-sidecar outcome of ok | degraded | unavailable, plus warnings. FAILURE IS NON-FATAL, matching today's behavior, but is NEVER silently swallowed - every degradation produces a warning and a SidecarProbed event. All invocations use argv arrays with resolved absolute executable paths; no shell (H4). CRITICAL (compound learning 2026-08-08-state-vs-call-outcome-conflation-in-telemetry-mapping): a sidecar's reported STATE is NOT the supervisor call's own outcome - a degraded Engram must remain a per-sidecar outcome and must never be mapped onto the supervisor's status. AUTHORITY (H6), SCOPE NARROWED BY THE F20 RULING: the prohibition is on DOMAIN/AUTHORITY MUTATION, not on derived-index maintenance. PROHIBITED: creating, updating, archiving or deleting any backlog artifact, shipment or checkpoint; writing Engram authority records; granting Engram execution or decision authority; letting any supervisor decision depend on a sidecar's reported state. EXPLICITLY PERMITTED, and in fact REQUIRED because they are today's behavior: `backlogit sync` and Engram pre-warm/`bind`, both of which only rebuild a DERIVED LOCAL INDEX from an unchanged source of truth and create no domain facts. The earlier blanket phrasing ('backlogit and graphtor are not mutated by this service') contradicted the mandate to run those very operations and is withdrawn. A test asserts the service performs no backlog-artifact write and no Engram authority write, while permitting the two index operations by name.
