Author workflow policy P-021 and carry it coherently across every autoharness surface that governs a bug-fix or Copilot-review-comment fix cycle, so that an out-of-scope expansion is never implemented in that cycle and is instead captured as a deferred stash entry requiring mandatory Stage deliberation.

<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Source stash: B48A482A. Deliberation: 019-DL.

Operator direction: whenever fixing a bug or resolving a Copilot review comment would expand work beyond the currently approved feature/task scope, the expansion MUST NOT be implemented in that fix cycle. It must be captured as a separate stash entry for later Stage triage and MANDATORY deliberation/research before any planning or implementation. The active feature/task remains bounded. Review pressure, severity, dark mode, and convenience do not authorize silent scope expansion. The original in-scope defect/comment is still resolved as far as possible without the expansion, with the deferred expansion ID referenced in the review reply and residual-risk record.

This feature authors P-021 in the workflow policy registry (Amendment Log 1.20.0) and carries clauses C1-C7 across the policy registry, three agent templates, four shared instructions, two skill templates, one prompt, eight dogfood artifacts with refreshed manifest checksums, and a new contract test.

It also repairs a latent P-010 contradiction: Ship's Role Boundary currently forbids 'stash operations' while Ship pre-merge Step 9 and post-merge Step 6 already require them. Without that repair, the mandated capture would be a policy violation by construction.
<!-- END:description -->
