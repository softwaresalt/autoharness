BINDING OPERATOR DIRECTIVE
A shipment manifest is a FLAT MANIFEST of exactly what is delivered. It is NOT a container and carries NO implicit hierarchical closure semantics. A feature MAY be decomposed across multiple shipments; the FINAL shipment for that feature carries the feature item itself as the FINAL manifest entry. Items under a feature that are excluded, removed, descoped, rejected, merged into other work, or otherwise absent from a shipment MUST NEVER block the feature record or shipment record from being marked shipped solely because of their ancestry. Closure applies ONLY to explicitly listed manifest items plus the shipment record; hierarchy remains useful for planning/traceability but MUST NOT expand closure scope.
