The catalog is a TYPED REGISTRY, not a bare string list: each entry carries a STABLE ACTION IDENTIFIER that is part of the module's public contract and MUST NOT be renamed silently once shipped, plus the metadata the approval service needs to render and resolve a request without inventing policy of its own - at minimum a human-readable summary, the permitted options, the SAFE DEFAULT used for non-interactive H2 fail-closed resolution, and the timeout. It is DECLARED ONCE HERE AND NOWHERE ELSE: no downstream task may define a second list, and duplication is itself a defect. EXHAUSTIVENESS IS ENFORCED HERE, NOT ONLY DOWNSTREAM: this task's tests assert the catalog is NON-EMPTY, that every entry has a unique stable identifier and complete required metadata, and that the registry is CLOSED - lookup of an unregistered identifier RAISES rather than returning a permissive default - so an unknown gated action FAILS CLOSED at the contract boundary itself. THIS TASK IS NOT DONE UNTIL THE CATALOG IS DEFINED AND EXPORTED FROM contracts.py, because 120.004-T's approval-wiring acceptance criteria are unsatisfiable without it, and satisfying them by editing this already-completed upstream task would be out of scope.
