<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Goal: the engine-semantics and close-path-selection record codec, split out of A1 (192.001-T) by Copilot PR #481 T8.
Files: src/autoharness/gates/cascade_evidence.py (extended), tests/test_cascade_evidence_codec_selection.py (new).
Functions (4): engine_semantics_to_record, engine_semantics_from_record, close_path_selection_to_record, close_path_selection_from_record.
Key rules:
- engine_semantics_* encode an EngineSemanticsDecision plus the invocation_surface input as {verdict, reason, probed_version, minor_line ([major, minor] or null), probed_commit, probe_surface, invocation_surface}. There is no probe excerpt (R7; the probe stdout excerpt is tool.version_excerpt). No field is redacted (R6): values are stored verbatim so re-assessment reproduces them.
- engine_semantics_from_record rebuilds the decision fields and invocation_surface; a missing key, a minor_line that is not null or a two-integer list, or an unknown verdict raises CascadeEvidenceError.
- close_path_selection_* encode the (ClosePath, reason) result of select_close_path as {selected_close_path: cascade | safe_close, reason}; an unknown selected_close_path raises CascadeEvidenceError.
- A1's selection-consistency check decodes the recorded engine_semantics and close_path_selection through these helpers (one decoder) before re-assessing with assess_cascade_engine_semantics and select_close_path.
- Round trips are stable; comparisons always compare *_to_record encodings.
