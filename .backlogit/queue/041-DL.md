# cascade-close durable journaling, detach/poll, and --recover with provenance (214-S incident)

Full analysis: docs/decisions/2026-10-16-cascade-close-recovery-and-journaling-deliberation.md

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
