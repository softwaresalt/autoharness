Problem: P-015 permits the destructive backlogit shipment ship cascade only when classify_shipment_close_path returns CASCADE, but the audit trail (fresh classifier re-run, out-of-manifest fingerprint, raw close output, INV-10 checks) is agent-discretionary prose in the shipment-reconcile Cascade Close Sub-Procedure. During the 175-S close none of it survived, and the evidence cannot be reproduced after a cascade. The write-time closure-evidence gate does not know which close path ran, so a missing record is never refused.

Solution: a new autoharness-owned command, autoharness shipment cascade-close (package src/autoharness/shipment_close/; read-only contract and validator in src/autoharness/gates/cascade_evidence.py). Under a per-pair O_EXCL lock it re-runs the classifier fresh, fingerprints the shipment record, manifest members, out-of-manifest descendants and linked deliberations, writes an owner-bound evidence record pre_close -> invoking -> post_close atomically at docs/closure/evidence/{S}-{F}-close-evidence.json (streamed capture bounded at 1 MiB / 10,000 lines, persisted redacted excerpts of 64 KiB / 500 lines plus SHA-256), invokes backlogit through a fixed argv with shell=False and a process-group timeout, evaluates INV-10 postconditions purely, and exits with one fixed table (0/2/3/4/5/6/7/8). --classify-only records the verdict (CASCADE or SAFE_CLOSE) without mutating. The closure-evidence gate then requires close_path and close_evidence on every new closure artifact and fails closed without a valid record. The skills and the Ship agent route every close through the command; a direct backlogit shipment ship or backlogit_ship_shipment call becomes a P-005 deviation.
