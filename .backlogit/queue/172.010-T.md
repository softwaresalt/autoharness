GREEN POST-ACTIVATION - STRUCTURAL VERIFICATION OF THE NOW-WIRED SYSTEM. A structural assertion may never precede the wiring it asserts, which is why this task follows the atomic activation and no reverse edge exists.

ASSERT, OVER THE ACTIVATED SYSTEM:
1. THE INVENTORY RECONCILES. Every bounded producer marker corresponds one-to-one with an entry in schemas/checkpoint-producer-inventory.json - a marker without an entry fails, an entry without a marker fails - and EXACTLY ONE entry (the adapter implementation) is raw-allowed.
2. NO SURFACE OUTSIDE THE ADAPTER REACHES THE RAW CREATE OPERATION. This is asserted through the TYPED INVENTORY AND BOUNDED MARKERS, never through a broad free-text scan: a text scan cannot distinguish a PROHIBITION DOCUMENT that names the raw operation in order to forbid it from an ACTUAL PRODUCER DECLARATION that reaches it, and it fails in both directions. The discriminating fixtures are required - a prohibition-reference occurrence PROVEN NOT TO BE A VIOLATION, and a SYNTHETIC THIRD PRODUCER carrying a real marker with no inventory entry that MUST FAIL. A verifier that cannot be made to fail proves nothing.
3. EVERY REGISTRY AND EVERY AGENT TOOL DECLARATION - installed and template - maps the GUARDED operation. The surface set is DERIVED BY ENUMERATION, not hardcoded to the registries known today; a gate hardcoded to today's surfaces passes silently the moment a new one is added.
4. INSTALLED AND TEMPLATE COPIES AGREE for every activated surface, so the installed copy and the template it is generated from cannot drift.
5. THE AUTHORITATIVE CHECKPOINT PAYLOAD CONTRACT no longer advertises the raw create operation as a harness producer path.
6. THE INSTALL MANIFEST REPORTS NO DRIFT: the checksum recorded for every tracked file modified by the activation matches that file's current content. Where a modified surface is not manifest-tracked, the test records that explicitly rather than assuming it.
