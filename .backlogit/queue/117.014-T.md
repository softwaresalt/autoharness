Emits a CopilotResolved event. Pure resolution + composition: this module does not spawn. Returns a typed ResolveResult carrying the absolute executable path and the composed argv list. Tests cover the full resolution precedence matrix, the --remote truthiness table including the double-add guard, verbatim passthrough of arguments containing spaces/quotes/leading dashes, and the unresolvable-executable fatal path.
