WORKSTREAM 1 (6D62077C) — paired-edit maintenance contract
Stage's read-only spike falsified the stash entry's own premise. The divergence is BIDIRECTIONAL, not conditional stripping: for the _ship pair, 508 of 692 dogfood lines (73%) are absent from the rendered template AND 697 of 880 rendered lines (79%) are absent from the dogfood file. Decision: do NOT extend _render_template — formally define these pairs as paired-edit maintained and pin the set.

WORKSTREAM 2 (8D570CF8) — deprecated-operation migration
backlogit has deprecated backlogit_stash_remove in favour of backlogit_stash_archive (verified against v1.10.0: the MCP manifest exposes backlogit_stash_archive as the primary archive operation AND backlogit_stash_remove self-described as "[Deprecated: use backlogit_stash_archive]"; the CLI exposes `backlogit stash archive`, with `stash remove` retained only as an alias resolving to the same archive handler). Ship's post-merge Step 7 still prescribes the deprecated operation. Priority stays low (no correctness impact today) but is non-deferrable: the deprecated name asserts removal semantics for what is actually a non-destructive archival, and a deprecated surface is not a durable execution target. The harness's standard MCP-first/CLI-fallback degradation pattern is FULLY SATISFIABLE today - MCP primary backlogit_stash_archive, CLI fallback `backlogit stash archive` - and the contract must name that pair rather than the deprecated operation.
