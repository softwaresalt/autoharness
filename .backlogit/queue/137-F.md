Covering feature for the operator-designated "two harness-consistency follow-ups": stash 6D62077C (medium, spike) and stash 8D570CF8 (low, task). Both are P-021 C2 DEFERRED SCOPE EXPANSION captures from shipment 143-S / feature 134-F.

WHY THESE SHIP TOGETHER
templates/agents/_ship.agent.md.tmpl <-> .github/agents/_ship.agent.md is one of the four divergent template/dogfood pairs that 6D62077C analyses. The 8D570CF8 migration IS a paired edit on that very pair, so it must be performed under the maintenance contract 6D62077C settles. Ordering within the shipment is therefore mandatory: the contract lands first, the migration second.

WORKSTREAM 1 (6D62077C) — paired-edit maintenance contract
Stage's read-only spike falsified the stash entry's own premise. The divergence is BIDIRECTIONAL, not conditional stripping: for the _ship pair, 508 of 692 dogfood lines (73%) are absent from the rendered template AND 697 of 880 rendered lines (79%) are absent from the dogfood file. Three distinct causes were isolated (install-time conditional content; semantic prose drift; variable-derivation coverage gap). Decision: do NOT extend _render_template — formally define these pairs as paired-edit maintained and pin the set.

WORKSTREAM 2 (8D570CF8) — deprecated-operation migration
backlogit has deprecated backlogit_stash_remove in favour of backlogit_stash_archive, and the CLI exposes no `stash remove` subcommand at all (verified against v1.10.0). Ship's post-merge Step 7 still prescribes the deprecated operation. Priority stays low (no correctness impact today) but is non-deferrable: the harness's standard MCP-first/CLI-fallback degradation pattern has NO fallback for this operation, so a degraded session leaves Step 7 with no route to completion.
