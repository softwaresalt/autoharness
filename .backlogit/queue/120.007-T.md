SIZE: M. COMPLEXITY: high. (Two independent axes; also emitted as structured backlog fields.) Shipment 3 (P1). HIGHEST BLAST RADIUS IN THE FEATURE. Reduce start.ps1 and start.sh - and their templates/ counterparts, so generated workspaces are not left with orphaned inline policy (H9.5) - to thin compatibility shims that locate the interpreter/entry point and exec `autoharness run -- <args>`. NO POLICY DUPLICATION MAY SURVIVE in PowerShell or bash: no .env.local parsing, no token logic, no COPILOT_HOME/ENGRAM_DATA_DIR defaulting, no sidecar calls, no exe resolution, no --remote decision. H3: the child's exit status must propagate verbatim through the shim - `|| true`, `-ErrorAction SilentlyContinue`, and any pipeline that masks the exit status around the child launch are PROHIBITED and asserted against. ACCEPTANCE CRITERION (H1, non-negotiable): the characterization suites are re-run BYTE-IDENTICAL - no test file may be edited to accommodate the migration; a required assertion change is escalated as an operator product decision. ROLLBACK: preserve the pre-migration scripts by git SHA in the migration doc so rollback is a single-file revert per shim, and add an AUTOHARNESS_SUPERVISOR=0 escape hatch that makes the shim execute the legacy inline path without a redeploy.
