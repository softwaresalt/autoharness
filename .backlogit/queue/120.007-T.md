Reduce start.ps1 and start.sh - and their templates/ counterparts, so generated workspaces are not left with orphaned inline policy (H9.5) - to thin compatibility shims that locate the interpreter/entry point and exec `autoharness run -- <args>`. NO POLICY DUPLICATION MAY SURVIVE in PowerShell or bash: no .env.local parsing, no token logic, no COPILOT_HOME/ENGRAM_DATA_DIR defaulting, no sidecar calls, no exe resolution, no --remote decision. H3: the child's exit status must propagate verbatim through the shim - `|| true`, `-ErrorAction SilentlyContinue`, and any pipeline that masks the exit status around the child launch are PROHIBITED and asserted against. TERMINAL ATTACHMENT MUST ALSO SURVIVE THE SHIM (F29): the bash shim uses exec and the PowerShell shim inherits handles, so the child remains attached to the operator's terminal; the byte-identical characterization suites include the TTY assertions added by 118.001-T and 118.002-T. ACCEPTANCE CRITERION (H1, non-negotiable, SCOPED BY THE P1-B RULING 2026-08-12): the characterization suites are re-run BYTE-IDENTICAL FOR EVERY UNCHANGED SCENARIO - no test file may be edited to accommodate the migration, and any unlisted assertion change is escalated as an operator product decision. EXACTLY TWO APPROVED DELTAS ARE CARVED OUT, BOTH LANDING ON POSIX (P1-2 ruling, 2026-08-12 - THIS SUPERSEDES THE EARLIER "EXACTLY ONE DELTA" WORDING, WHICH WAS FALSE AND IS WITHDRAWN). DELTA 1 - PAT-WITHOUT-gh BECOMES NON-FATAL: the legacy assertion that an absent gh makes GITHUB_PERSONAL_ACCESS_TOKEN assignment ERROR is BASELINE EVIDENCE, NOT A REQUIREMENT, and the claim that this scenario must remain byte-identical is WITHDRAWN; assert instead the 120.001-T target behavior - with gh absent, bootstrap COMPLETES NON-FATALLY, emits an explicit warning and a structured event naming the missing tool and the unset variable, leaves GITHUB_PERSONAL_ACCESS_TOKEN UNSET (not set-empty), propagates a success exit status, and LEAKS NO SECRET VALUE OR FRAGMENT into the warning, event, journal, SupervisorResult.data or telemetry. DELTA 2 - POSIX GAINS THE SHARED BOOTSTRAP POLICY IT PREVIOUSLY LACKED: 118.002-T pins, as observable ABSENCES of today's start.sh, BOTH "no ENGRAM_DATA_DIR default" AND "no GITHUB_PERSONAL_ACCESS_TOKEN", while 120.001-T is the SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH for bootstrap policy and deliberately supplies both on every platform - so after migration POSIX necessarily gains (a) the deterministic workspace-local ENGRAM_DATA_DIR default <root>/.engram, honoring a pre-set value, and (b) PAT resolution via gh auth token, non-fatal in the gh-absent and gh-failing cases per DELTA 1. THOSE TWO POSIX ABSENCE ASSERTIONS ARE THEREFORE REPLACED, NOT PRESERVED, and re-asserting them as still-absent after migration is PROHIBITED; assert positively that POSIX sets ENGRAM_DATA_DIR to the documented default while honoring a pre-set value, and that with gh PRESENT the PAT is resolved and set on POSIX exactly as on Windows. WINDOWS/POSIX TARGET SEMANTICS ARE EXPLICITLY IDENTICAL AND MUST BE TESTED AS SUCH: because 120.001-T is the sole authority, the target contract carries NO platform branch for .env.local precedence, COPILOT_HOME/ENGRAM_DATA_DIR defaulting, or token resolution - assert the SAME observable outcomes on both platforms for each, and treat a platform-conditional expectation as a defect unless it corresponds to a documented OS constraint. DO NOT HIDE THE PLATFORM DIFFERENCE BY RE-INTRODUCING POSIX-SPECIFIC BEHAVIOR INTO THE SHARED SERVICE - the pre-migration divergence was an accident of two hand-maintained scripts, and unifying it is the point of Shipment 3. THE CARVE-OUT IS EXHAUSTIVE AND SCENARIO-SCOPED: these two deltas are the ONLY approved behavior changes, and every other characterization assertion - including the TTY assertions above, exit-status propagation, argv forwarding, exe resolution order and --remote gating - remains BYTE-IDENTICAL on both platforms, with any further assertion change escalated as an operator product decision.
