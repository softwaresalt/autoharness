CARRIER ROLES. A clause is not carried the same way on every surface, and asserting one identical marker across a whole row would demand text that some carriers are contractually forbidden to contain (hardening H5 requires most surfaces to REFERENCE the registry rather than paraphrase it) or that their subject matter cannot support (a CI surface has no review thread to reply on). Every carrier below therefore declares a ROLE, and the clause-coverage test asserts the marker appropriate to that role:

- `[A]` AUTHORITATIVE - authors the full normative clause text. Only `workflow-policies.md.tmpl`, for all seven clauses.
- `[N]` NORMATIVE RESTATEMENT - restates clause text in operational form. Permitted ONLY where a task explicitly designates it (circuit-breaker for C1; the Ship Role Boundary and role-enforcement for C5).
- `[P]` PROCEDURAL - implements the clause as a required step, gate, or Stop-Conditions annotation in a workflow, citing P-021 by ID and clause label per H5. A procedural carrier is asserted on the STEP it must contain, never on the registry's clause prose.
- `[G]` GUARD-ONLY - carries only the C3 symmetric sub-guard (H12) and nothing else of that clause.
