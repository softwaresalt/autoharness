<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Add a bounded retention window: keep at most max_retained_segments sealed segments, pruning the oldest sealed segment(s) whenever a rollover would exceed the window, so total on-disk usage is bounded by (max_retained_segments + 1) * max_segment_bytes. Replay/conflict checks continue to scan only still-retained segments. Explicitly document and test the retention-horizon bound: once the segment carrying an epoch_id is pruned, a later replay of that epoch_id can no longer be detected and may be appended as new (acceptable -- JSONL is a best-effort mirror; SQLite is authoritative). Depends on 095.002-T (rollover). Files: src/autoharness/telemetry/jsonl_sink.py, tests/test_telemetry_jsonl_sink.py. <=3 files, <=5 functions, <=4 test scenarios. TDD.
