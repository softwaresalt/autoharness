Correct the autoharness P-015 / shipment-reconcile cascade-close `archived_ids` post-condition so it asserts something true of the Backlogit engine, without weakening either safety property it provides.

PROBLEM. `templates/policies/workflow-policies.md.tmpl` (P-015 fully-covered-root exception item 7) and `templates/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md.tmpl` (cascade-close step 3) assert a FULL-SET EQUALITY over `archived_ids` -- "nothing more, nothing less", evaluated "against the manifest's full item set", and pinned as a rule that "must never be relaxed". That claim is FALSE. `archived_ids` is a TRANSITION LOG: it reports artifacts ACTUALLY TRANSITIONED to archived during that invocation. A truly `status: archived` member has no transition to report and is correctly omitted. The gate therefore forces a spurious P-005 HALT on any legitimate cascade close whose manifest contains a pre-archived task.

EVIDENCE (engine source, read-only, backlogit @ b0772938): `internal/core/shipment_lifecycle.go` `archiveItems()` L1130 `if item.Status == models.StatusArchived { continue }` -- skipped AND not appended to the slice that becomes `ArchivedIDs` (L670, L709); same exclusion at L799/L808. Empirically corroborated by 154-S and 153-S logs.

The 2026-08-18 spike that manufactured the false invariant was METHODOLOGICALLY FLAWED: it built its "pre-archived" arms with `move --status done`, which relocates the record but leaves declared `status: done`. Against the L1130 guard `done != archived`, so all three arms were, at the guard that matters, the control arm.

CORRECTED CONTRACT. Snapshot DECLARED status at Step 0(b) (never directory location, never a post-close read), then add the declared pre-close statuses of qualifying feature members identified by Step 0(c). `allowed_ids` = manifest tasks + qualifying feature members + shipment record. `required_ids` = the shipment record unconditionally + every other allowed artifact NOT truly `status: archived` in that pre-close status map; never derive it only by iterating Step 0(b)'s manifest entries. FAIL when `archived_ids - allowed_ids` is non-empty; FAIL when `required_ids - archived_ids` is non-empty; an already-archived allowed member may be included or omitted. PRESERVE persisted-final-state, `returned_ids`, parent-id, shipment-status, protected-set, and unexpected-ID checks at full strength.

NON-GOALS: no Backlogit engine change; no fresh spike; no relaxation of out-of-scope ID detection; no cascade-selection / safe-close / lifecycle change; no Backlogit-side blocker; no rewriting of the 154-S closure record or changelog history.
