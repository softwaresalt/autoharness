templates/agents/review/security-reviewer.agent.md.tmpl carries a suppression rule excluding findings not tied to the features declared purpose, which can hide a concrete vulnerability introduced by the diff BEFORE it is ever reported to the coordinator for disposition. Remove the purpose-based exclusion.
