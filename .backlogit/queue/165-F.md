CONTRACT (decision D1). Predecessors derive EXCLUSIVELY from ShipmentState.blocking_predecessor_ids. When that set is empty, exactly one of three states applies, each with its own provenance value: DECLARED ROOT (record carries the `dag-root` label) passes as `declared_root`; GENESIS passes as `genesis` under the narrow rule below; UNSEQUENCED blocks with token UNSEQUENCED_SHIPMENT and a message naming both remedies. Non-empty edge sets evaluate as `explicit`. The four states are total and mutually exclusive, and `predecessor_source` appears on every payload, blocked and passed alike.

GENESIS IS THE SOLE-RECORD RULE (PR review-fix cycle 2, thread PRRT_kwDORzpWpM6h3frF — this REPLACES the cycle-2 three-probe G2/G3/G4 formulation, which REPLACED the cycle-1 "no shipped-terminal shipment exists" rule). Genesis is granted only when BOTH hold:
  G1 — the candidate declares no `blocks` edges and carries no `dag-root` label; AND
  G2 — SOLE RECORD: the candidate is the ONLY shipment record that exists in the workspace, counting LIVE AND ARCHIVED records together, REGARDLESS OF STATUS AND REGARDLESS OF PROVENANCE.
ANY other shipment record disqualifies genesis — whatever its status, recognized or not. WHY THE THREE-PROBE FORM WAS A FAIL-OPEN: cycle 2's G3 enumerated the disqualifying nonterminal states as "queued or active" and its G2/G4 covered only `shipped` and `abandoned`, so ANY value outside those three enumerations cleared all three probes at once and returned `genesis` — an unearned pass, the exact fail-open direction this feature exists to close. Enumerating statuses is inherently fragile: every future value added to the vocabulary silently re-opens the hole. COUNTING RECORDS IS NOT: genesis means "this workspace contains exactly one shipment record", which cannot be widened by any later vocabulary change. ENUM CORRECTION (PR review-fix cycle 3, thread PRRT_kwDORzpWpM6h3ttS): the cycle-2 text illustrated this with a `blocked` shipment and called `blocked` a member of the shipment status enum. That is FALSE and is RETRACTED — the current vocabulary is EXACTLY {`queued`, `active`, `shipped`, `abandoned`}, and `blocked` on a shipment is MALFORMED LEGACY DATA. The fail-open finding and the sole-record replacement both STAND, and are in fact WIDER than cycle 2 claimed: the hole admitted every unenumerated value, not just one. `blocked` also SPLITS by location — a LIVE `blocked` record raises BacklogUnavailableError at reader time (existing malformed-input fail-closed behaviour, never reaching `unsequenced`), while an ARCHIVED `archived_status: blocked` record counts as a disqualifying unclassifiable record and the candidate resolves to `unsequenced`.
FAIL CLOSED ON ANYTHING UNREADABLE (same thread). A shipment record whose status is ABSENT, empty, non-string, unparseable, or NOT A RECOGNIZED MEMBER of the current four-value vocabulary — including an unrecognized `archived_status` on an archived record — STILL COUNTS AS A DISQUALIFYING RECORD. It is NEVER skipped, NEVER defaulted to a benign value, and NEVER treated as absent: a record that cannot be classified is precisely the record whose sequencing significance is unknown, and discarding it is the fail-open move. If the live or archive record set cannot be ENUMERATED at all, the evaluation FAILS CLOSED (BacklogUnavailableError) rather than concluding sole-extancy from a short read.
Genesis remains a CARDINALITY-1 state: it cannot hold for two shipments at once, and it ceases to be available the moment a workspace holds a second shipment record of any kind. From that point on, every intended root must be DECLARED or it blocks as `unsequenced`. The sole-record fact is computed ONCE per evaluation from a single snapshot and shared by both gates.
