# Automatic, fail-closed CASCADE close-evidence capture (175-S evidence gap)

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
CASCADE close evidence (the fresh classifier re-check, the out-of-manifest snapshot, and the raw backlogit shipment ship output) is captured by agent-discretionary prose in the shipment-reconcile Cascade Close Sub-Procedure. During the 175-S close (5cc5371a), none of it survived, and closure PR #459 halted BLOCKED, needing operator deviation operator-accepts-175s-cascade-evidence-deviation. The evidence cannot be reproduced after a cascade. The closure-evidence gate does not know which close path ran. Full analysis: docs/decisions/2026-09-27-close-evidence-frontmatter-context-tier-staging-deliberation.md (F-A1 to F-A6).
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
(1) An autoharness-owned mutating command, `autoharness shipment cascade-close`, plus closure-evidence gate enforcement [chosen]. (2) Upstream-only: wait for backlogit to emit evidence from shipment ship [rejected: it blocks a high-priority bug on another repository]. (3) Depend on 179-F/185-S safe primitives [rejected: 185-S is unclaimable by construction behind the withheld 184-S]. (4) More prose checklists in the skill [rejected: prose is the root cause].
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
D-A1 through D-A6, as amended by plan review (Stage-recommended, pending operator confirmation). The wrapper lives in autoharness, and a portable upstream backlogit request does not block. The evidence JSON is committed at docs/closure/evidence/{S}-{F}-close-evidence.json and kept permanently, with circuit-breaker bounds (1 MiB/10k-line capture; 64 KiB/500-line persisted excerpt plus SHA-256) and redaction. SAFE_CLOSE gets a --classify-only verdict record, and the gate fails closed without a valid one (review cycle 1 amendment of D-A3, AN-F01). CASCADE fails closed without a valid post-close record. New closure artifacts declare close_path and close_evidence. The contract stays in code. Plan: docs/plans/2026-09-27-cascade-close-evidence-capture-plan.md (authoritative where this summary and the plan differ).
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->
