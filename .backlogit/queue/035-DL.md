# Automatic, fail-closed CASCADE close-evidence capture (175-S evidence gap)

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
CASCADE close evidence (the fresh classifier re-check, the out-of-manifest snapshot, and the raw backlogit shipment ship output) is captured by agent-discretionary prose in the shipment-reconcile Cascade Close Sub-Procedure. During the 175-S close (5cc5371a), none of it survived, and closure PR #459 halted BLOCKED, needing operator deviation operator-accepts-175s-cascade-evidence-deviation. Full analysis: docs/decisions/2026-09-27-close-evidence-frontmatter-context-tier-staging-deliberation.md (F-A1 to F-A6).
