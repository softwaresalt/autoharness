Define the typed V1 protocol contracts for the four-tier Observe/Steer/Approve/Privileged taxonomy, exposing only Observe and Steer remotely. Specify the closed structured observation and steering vocabulary, request identity, response/error shapes, and idempotency/state-machine semantics for pause/resume/cancel/request-checkpoint.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
- Observe and Steer commands are exhaustively enumerated and unknown/arbitrary/raw commands fail closed.
- Approve and Privileged are represented as local-only and cannot be remotely dispatched.
- Pause, resume, cancel, and request-checkpoint semantics define valid transitions, duplicate handling, and errors.
- Every remote command has a maximum encoded request size of 16 KiB and a per-binding rate limit of 30 requests per rolling minute with a burst of at most 5; oversize and over-limit requests fail closed before dispatch with structured errors, and the limits are covered by contract tests.
- No general-purpose execution API or raw component exposure is introduced.

<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

<!-- BEGIN:implementation-notes -->
Preserve Copilot CLI as the execution engine. Plan 2 adds a bounded control plane only. The limits above apply to all Observe and Steer remote messages, including reconnect/control metadata; 121.002-T must enforce the same limits before state-machine dispatch.
<!-- END:implementation-notes -->
