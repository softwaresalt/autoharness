- SOLE RECORD: how many shipment records exist in the workspace in total, counting LIVE AND ARCHIVED records together, REGARDLESS OF STATUS AND REGARDLESS OF PROVENANCE. Genesis requires that count to be exactly ONE — the candidate itself.
