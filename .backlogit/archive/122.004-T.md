title: Harden account, workspace, session, and capability authorization policy
updated_at: 2026-08-14T23:46:50.1333053Z
---

Define the application authorization boundary over authenticated Dev Tunnel reachability. Bind requests to one enrolled account/principal when trusted tunnel identity metadata is available, plus workspace and session binding. Add capability-scoped policy and a local-approval handoff for yolo/permission broadening, credential-affecting actions, restart/shutdown, or other privileged operations. Fail closed on identity absence/mismatch, stale tokens, workspace/session mismatch, revoked binding, and unknown capabilities.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
Valid enrolled principal/session requests succeed; wrong account, missing/unverifiable principal, wrong workspace/session, stale/future token, revoked binding, and unknown capability fail closed and journal security metadata only; Observe and bounded Steer permissions are distinct; privileged requests are queued for local approval and cannot self-authorize; no credential or raw text appears in audit; threat-model tests cover tunnel/auth unavailable and local revoke.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Do not introduce a second identity provider or trust tunnel URL secrecy. Preserve the local console as the absolute authority.
<!-- END:description -->

<!-- BEGIN:implementation-notes -->
Keep Dev Tunnel as the reachability/auth mechanism selected for Plan 2 V1, while making account-boundary verification an explicit adapter contract rather than implicit trust.
<!-- END:implementation-notes -->
