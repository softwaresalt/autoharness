Manifest-only or invalid config keeps the existing manifest-first setdefault precedence and emits no stale warning. Every non-route variable keeps manifest-first precedence.
- When a recorded route variable differs from its config-derived value, append a non-fatal warnings[] entry ROUTE_VARIABLE_STALE: <VAR> recorded=<x> config=<y>. The dogfood manifest records no route variables, so this repository's render is unchanged.
