This is the P-006 H2 task and it is the most delicate part of the feature, because docs/dag-readiness-gate.md currently states a PERMANENT NON-GOAL that the gate will never "Select or execute a 'next' shipment automatically."

REQUIRED RECONCILIATION — the existing non-goal sentence STAYS IN FORCE and MUST NOT be deleted, weakened, or quietly reworded. Add a clearly labelled subsection that draws the distinction explicitly:
- PROHIBITED, PERMANENTLY: automatic selection-FOR-EXECUTION — claiming, activating, or executing a shipment without an explicit human/Ship decision; and any scheduler or parallel execution (P-001/P-016).
- DELIVERED HERE: a deterministic, READ-ONLY RECOMMENDATION that a human or the Orchestrator must still act on explicitly. It claims nothing, activates nothing, mutates nothing, creates no branch or worktree, and does not authorize a claim. Ship's claim authority is untouched. A null cursor never authorizes a claim either.
Also update the existing sentence that says Phase 2 "remains operator-gated and deferred in the living tracker (stash 33CC445C)" — Phase 2 is delivered as of this feature; point it at 115-F and 014-DL.

DOCUMENT — the SEVEN-outcome resolution order in evaluation order, numbered 1-7: (1) `degraded`, (2) `cycle_detected`, (3) `ambiguous_provenance`, (4) `multi_active_anomaly`, (5) `resume_active`, (6) `ready_set_head`, (7) `no_candidates`. State the OWNERSHIP SPLIT explicitly and do NOT describe a seven-branch analyzer: outcome 1 `degraded` is synthesized by the CLI in the `BacklogUnavailableError` handler BEFORE the analyzer is invoked (on that path the shipment enumeration and the readiness result do not exist, so the analyzer cannot be called and cannot observe degradation); outcomes 2-7 are the analyzer's SIX branches. Also document: the DESC-fan-out then ASC-id total-order tie-break, WHY it is total, and that it applies to branch 6 (`ready_set_head`) ONLY and never to branch 5 (`resume_active`); the anomaly-first evaluation structure; and the three new `--json` fields added to the output-shape table with the full seven-value reason enum and the always-present `candidate_ids`/`offending_ids` detail keys, which are ALWAYS arrays on every path (the degraded path shows exactly `{"candidate_ids": [], "offending_ids": []}` — never `{}`, never null).

INTEROP CONTRACT (must be stated prominently): `next_eligible` is NOT guaranteed to be a member of `ready_set`. Under `resume_active` the cursor is an `active` shipment, and `ready_set` by definition contains only live `queued` shipments. Consumers MUST use `next_eligible_reason` as the discriminator and MUST NOT treat non-membership as an error.

Also state that exit codes are unchanged and that a multi-active anomaly is REPORTED here but BLOCKED by the pipeline-topology gate, which remains the owner of that verdict.
