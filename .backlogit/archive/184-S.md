WHY THE DEPENDENCY EDGE WAS NOT ENOUGH. This shipment declared depends_on 182-S, the operation-transport spike. A blocks edge is an ORDERING mechanism and is NOT an AUTHORIZATION mechanism: it clears on PREDECESSOR COMPLETION. 182-S's sole verdict emitter 176.005-T REACHES COMPLETION ON ALL THREE OF ITS TOKENS - TRANSPORT_DECIDED, TRANSPORT_UNDECIDED and TRANSPORT_NOT_OBSERVED - and TWO OF THOSE ARE NON-AUTHORIZING. So on the edge alone this shipment would have become claimable the moment 182-S shipped, WHATEVER the spike concluded, including a TRANSPORT_UNDECIDED that names unanswered findings and a TRANSPORT_NOT_OBSERVED that means the spike never ran. 176.005-T states the rule that was not enforced: "TRANSPORT_UNDECIDED BLOCKS 184-S HARVEST: it does not degrade to a partial pass, and an unanswered finding is never inherited by 184-S as an assumption."

NO INSTALLED CLAIM GATE CONSUMES THE TOKEN. The repository has no shipment-claim predicate that reads docs/spikes/2026-09-18-autoharness-operation-transport-findings.md. The task-level fail-closed first-action reads that exist elsewhere in this portfolio, such as 169.015-T's, are INTRA-shipment gates between two tasks of one unit; they are not shipment-claim gates and cannot gate a successor shipment. Until such a predicate is genuinely installed, the only fail-closed representation is that THE SUCCESSOR RECORDS DO NOT EXIST IN THE EXECUTABLE QUEUE.

NO INVENTED STATUS. These records are withheld by ARCHIVAL, which is a repository-supported backlog operation. A live shipment carrying status blocked is NOT used: the pre_claim topology gate's live shipment status vocabulary is exactly queued, active, shipped and abandoned, and a live shipment record with blocked is malformed legacy data that fails closed at read time rather than deriving any sequencing state. Nothing is deleted and no evidence is destroyed; the full plan, feature and task text remain readable at .backlogit/archive/.

THE EXACT STAGE RE-HARVEST CONDITION. Stage - not Ship, and not an edge - re-harvests this unit if and ONLY if, in a NEW staging session, it reads docs/spikes/2026-09-18-autoharness-operation-transport-findings.md and observes EXACTLY ONE line beginning "COMPOSED_STATE: " whose token is the literal TRANSPORT_DECIDED, with absent=0 and an absent count that agrees with the required-findings ledger in the same artifact. ONLY THEN may Stage restore 178-F and 178.001-T through 178.006-T to the queue and re-create this shipment.

EVERY OTHER STATE HARVESTS NOTHING. TRANSPORT_UNDECIDED harvests NOTHING - there is no floor subset for this unit, because the substrate's transport shape IS the undecided question and a partial substrate would inherit the unanswered finding as an assumption. TRANSPORT_NOT_OBSERVED harvests NOTHING. A missing artifact, an artifact with no completed ledger, more than one COMPOSED_STATE line, an unrecognised token, and a verdict line whose absent count disagrees with the ledger ALL harvest NOTHING. Any of those requires a NEW Stage decision before any record returns, and no verdict short of TRANSPORT_DECIDED is a partial pass.
