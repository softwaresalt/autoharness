   *** REVISION-3 CORRECTION — THE COMPARISON DOMAIN IS THE PARSED SCALAR, NOT BYTES ***
   Revision 2 said the status was "stored verbatim" and compared "byte-for-byte", and treated unquoted `status: archived ` as a distinct non-inert value. THAT WAS UNIMPLEMENTABLE AND FACTUALLY WRONG, AND IS WITHDRAWN. Every record reaches this code through gates.topology._frontmatter, which parses the frontmatter with yaml.safe_load. A YAML load ERASES LEXICAL FORM: quoting style and trailing whitespace on a plain scalar are gone before any predicate can observe them, and YAML resolves `status: archived ` to exactly "archived".    ACCEPTED, DOCUMENTED LIMITATION: forms that are lexically distinct but YAML-EQUIVALENT collapse together and ARE inert — unquoted `status: archived` with trailing spaces, and `status: "archived"`, both parse to "archived". This is CORRECT, not a concession: the backlogit engine ALSO reads these records through a YAML parser, so the parsed scalar is the domain in which autoharness and the engine actually AGREE. A byte-level comparison would make autoharness disagree with the engine's own view of the record.
