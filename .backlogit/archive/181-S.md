WHY THE DEPENDENCY EDGE WAS NOT ENOUGH. This shipment declared depends_on 183-S, the conformance-isolation spike. A blocks edge is an ORDERING mechanism and is NOT an AUTHORIZATION mechanism: it clears on PREDECESSOR COMPLETION. 183-S's sole verdict emitter 177.006-T REACHES COMPLETION ON ALL FIVE OF ITS TOKENS - ISOLATION_NOT_OBSERVED, ISOLATION_UNDETERMINED, ISOLATION_CLEANUP_FAILED, ISOLATION_FLOOR_ONLY and ISOLATION_CHARACTERIZED - and only the last two authorize any harvest at all. So on the edge alone this shipment would have become claimable in FULL the moment 183-S shipped, whatever the spike concluded, including states in which the required isolation properties were never observed. 177.006-T already states the successor-eligibility rule that no installed mechanism enforced: under ISOLATION_CHARACTERIZED, 181-S may harvest every task the findings support; under ISOLATION_FLOOR_ONLY, 181-S may harvest ONLY the evidence-and-documentation floor tasks and NO task that assumes, relies on or asserts an unproven property; under ISOLATION_CLEANUP_FAILED, 181-S may harvest NOTHING.

THE PER-STATE TOKEN AND THE ALLOWED TASK SET WERE UNENFORCEABLE FROM A DEPENDENCY EDGE. An edge is a single boolean. It cannot express "this token admits all eleven tasks, that token admits only the floor subset, and three other tokens admit none", and it cannot select a subset of a manifest. A shipment whose manifest is fixed at harvest time CANNOT narrow itself in response to a verdict it has not yet read, so the only honest representation of a per-state allowed task set is to defer the harvest that fixes the manifest.

NO INSTALLED CLAIM GATE CONSUMES THE TOKEN. The repository has no shipment-claim predicate that reads docs/spikes/2026-09-18-conformance-isolation-findings.md. The task-level fail-closed first-action reads elsewhere in this portfolio are INTRA-shipment gates between two tasks of one unit and cannot gate a successor shipment. Until such a predicate is genuinely installed, the only fail-closed representation is that THE SUCCESSOR RECORDS DO NOT EXIST IN THE EXECUTABLE QUEUE.

NO INVENTED STATUS. These records are withheld by ARCHIVAL, a repository-supported backlog operation. A live shipment carrying status blocked is NOT used: the pre_claim topology gate's live shipment status vocabulary is exactly queued, active, shipped and abandoned, and a live shipment record with blocked is malformed legacy data that fails closed at read time. Nothing is deleted and no evidence is destroyed; the full plan, feature and task text remain readable at .backlogit/archive/.
