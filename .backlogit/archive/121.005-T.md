Add the V1 listener and tunnel lifecycle boundary: bind the application to loopback only, launch the authenticated devtunnel as the sole remote transport, and tear down cleanly on shutdown, tunnel loss, and crash paths. Fail clearly when devtunnel is unavailable or misconfigured, without adding a corporate-network fallback/preflight.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
- Non-loopback bind configuration is rejected or fails closed.
- Devtunnel is the only remote transport and uses its authenticated access control as the sole V1 auth mechanism.
- Tunnel creation requires an explicit local-console opt-in for the current supervisor session; it is never automatic, enabled by default, or persisted across sessions.
- Startup, loss, shutdown, and crash cleanup do not leave an exposed listener.
- Unavailable or misconfigured devtunnel produces a clear prerequisite/configuration error.
- No corporate-network fallback or separate preflight requirement is added.

<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->
