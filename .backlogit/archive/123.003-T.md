title: Add explicit restart handoff and binding-safe session recovery
updated_at: 2026-08-14T23:47:11.6415969Z
---

Define and implement controlled handoff when the supervisor or child restarts. Preserve local journal cursor authority, prevent stale remote tokens from controlling a replacement session, and support explicit local re-enrollment/reconnect with a bounded handoff descriptor. Do not silently transfer remote authority across a crash or workspace move.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
A clean child restart preserves the documented cursor/handoff state; a supervisor restart invalidates stale bindings and requires local re-enrollment; remote requests during handoff receive typed retry/unavailable results; old-session requests cannot reach the replacement child; local-only restart behavior remains compatible; tests cover crash, graceful restart, stale token, cursor resume, and unavailable journal.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Restart handoff is explicit and fail closed; it is not multi-session hosting.
<!-- END:description -->

<!-- BEGIN:implementation-notes -->
No remote retention store. Handoff records are local journal/session metadata only and never include secrets or raw operator text.
<!-- END:implementation-notes -->
