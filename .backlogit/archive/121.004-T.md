title: Bind remote requests to the local workspace and supervisor session
updated_at: 2026-08-14T08:25:00.6833832Z
---

Implement workspace/session binding for both Observe and Steer requests so a remote client cannot address a different local workspace or supervisor session. Preserve stateless clients while validating the local binding on every request.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
- Every request carries or resolves an explicit workspace/session binding.
- Mismatched, missing, expired, or ambiguous bindings fail closed.
- The binding does not grant Approve or Privileged authority.
- Stateless remote clients do not become a second source of session truth.

<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

<!-- BEGIN:implementation-notes -->
Threat-model workspace confusion and cross-session command delivery; use the local supervisor session and journal identity as authority.
<!-- END:implementation-notes -->
