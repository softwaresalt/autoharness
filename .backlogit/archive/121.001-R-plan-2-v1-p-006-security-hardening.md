Mandatory P-006 hardening artifact for 121-F. Threat-model the reduced V1 remote exposure without silently upgrading the accepted operator decisions.

<!-- BEGIN:decisions -->
Verdict: HARDENED. The plan is bounded to the operator-approved V1 Observe + Steer scope. All seven rulings are reflected. Residual risks and future boundaries are explicit. No implementation or Ship authorization is granted by this artifact.

<!-- END:decisions -->

<!-- BEGIN:findings -->
H1 Loopback-only bind: the service must never bind beyond loopback; reject or fail closed on non-loopback configuration.
H2 Closed authority vocabulary: expose only structured Observe and Steer commands; reject unknown, arbitrary, raw shell, and general-purpose component requests.
H3 Redaction and journal authority: redact secrets before output streaming; local journal is sole source of truth; remote clients are stateless; no remote retention store.
H4 Workspace/session binding: every observation and steer request is scoped to the intended local workspace and supervisor session; reject mismatches.
H5 Tunnel lifecycle/teardown: devtunnel startup, loss, shutdown, and crash cleanup must not leave an exposed listener; unavailable/misconfigured devtunnel fails clearly.
H6 Credential compromise: document containment, issuer-side revocation/replacement, and controlled supervisor restart as needed; do not treat application-layer verification as present in V1.
H7 Local-only authority: Observe and Steer are the only remotely exposed tiers; Approve and Privileged remain local-only; browser terminal streaming is permanently excluded.
H8 Accepted Q3 residual risk: devtunnel authenticated access control is the sole V1 auth mechanism. Application-layer identity verification is a future separately-decided follow-up, not a hidden hardening requirement.

<!-- END:findings -->

<!-- BEGIN:summary -->
HARDENING REQUIRED before plan review. Plan 2 exposes a local code-execution context and live credentials through a remote surface. The hardening scope is reduced V1 Observe + Steer, with devtunnel-only authenticated access as an explicitly accepted risk per Q3—not an omitted requirement.
