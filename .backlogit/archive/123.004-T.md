Expose a broker-level observation subscription/cursor mechanism that supports server-push where the presentation transport allows it and bounded long-poll fallback otherwise. Reuse the local event bus/journal catalog, redact before delivery, retain no separate remote transcript, drop slow-consumer data with explicit cursor/truncation signals, and never stall the supervised child.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
A client can subscribe from a cursor and receive ordered redacted events; reconnect resumes from the cursor or reports a bounded gap requiring journal refresh; slow/absent consumers do not block local execution; updates cover lifecycle, output, capability, control, approval, and agent-request events; long-poll timeout and unavailable journal are typed; tests prove automatic update behavior without repeated Tail clicks.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Automatic updates are a broker contract, not a Gradio polling hack.
<!-- END:description -->

<!-- BEGIN:implementation-notes -->
Keep the local journal as the sole source of truth and make all buffers bounded.
<!-- END:implementation-notes -->
