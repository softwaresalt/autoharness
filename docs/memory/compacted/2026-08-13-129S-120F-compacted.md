## Key Learnings (cross-references)

- `docs/compound/129-s-supervisor-engram-graphtor-startup-and-review-hardening.md`
  — full root cause, fix, and 8-round review lesson detail: (1) editor
  substitution variables are never resolved by headless/standalone CLI
  clients — verify with a literal-echo probe; (2) redaction coverage
  should be key-name-pattern-driven, not an enumerated allowlist; (3)
  every distinct output sink needs its own verified redaction point; (4)
  a lock's scope audit must trace every read of the protected state back
  to its logical origin, not just the write step; (5) a not-already-exited
  guard against signaling a process must be mirrored in every method
  capable of triggering that signal, not just the primary teardown path.
