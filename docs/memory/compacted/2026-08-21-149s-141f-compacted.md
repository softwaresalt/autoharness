source/test change) and removed ambient-cwd coupling from all 58
`tempfile.TemporaryDirectory(dir=Path.cwd())` sites across 4 test modules
(141.002-T/141.003-T/141.004-T). Added an AST-based structural guard.
Remediation of any confirmed polluter is 151-S's scope.

protocol; zero bisection rounds (premise disproven).

(discovered empirically after a static-read misclassification regressed a

throughout: zero assertion lines changed, isolation pass-count parity
confirmed per module.

(switched to `Path.rglob("*.py")`). Merged (2-parent commit confirmed).

(`024-DL`, plain `references` link) -- the THIRD occurrence of the
